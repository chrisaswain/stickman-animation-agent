# YouTube Optimization Reference

Guidelines for maximizing engagement on stickman animation videos.

## Pacing

| Duration | Scenes | Avg Scene Length | Target Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short (1-2 min) | 6-10 | 8-12s | Shorts, TikTok repost |
| Medium (3-5 min) | 15-25 | 10-15s | Standard YouTube |
| Long (5-10 min) | 25-50 | 12-18s | Deep topic, monetization |

## Hook Structure (First 5 Seconds)

1. **Pattern interrupt** — unexpected visual or statement
2. **Promise** — "By the end of this video, you'll know..."
3. **Relatable pain** — "You know that feeling when..."
4. **Curiosity gap** — start mid-action, explain later

The first scene MUST grab attention. No logos, no intros, no "hey guys."

## Scene Pacing Rules

- **Never hold a static frame > 4 seconds** — always have motion (character gesture, camera move, text reveal)
- **Visual change every 2-3 seconds** — new element, expression change, or camera move
- **Dialogue scenes** — alternate between speaker closeup and reaction shots
- **Montage scenes** — 1-2 seconds per image, rapid cuts
- **Comedic beats** — pause 0.5-1s AFTER the punchline for the laugh to land
- **Transitions** — 0.3-0.5s max, never longer

## Thumbnail Principles

- **One character, one emotion** — closeup face with exaggerated expression
- **High contrast** — bright colors, bold outlines
- **Max 3-4 words of text** — large, readable at phone size
- **Curiosity or emotion** — surprised face, pointing at something off-screen
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (1280x720 minimum)

## Title Patterns That Work

- "[Emotion] + [Topic]" — "Why Procrastination Is KILLING Your Dreams"
- "The [X] That Changed Everything" — narrative hook
- "I Tried [X] For 30 Days" — experiment format
- "What [Topic] Actually Looks Like" — relatable comedy
- Keep under 60 characters (truncation on mobile)

## Description Template

```
[Hook sentence matching the title]

[2-3 sentence summary of what the video covers]

Timestamps:
0:00 - [Section 1]
0:45 - [Section 2]
...

[Call to action — subscribe, comment topic requests]

#stickman #animation #[topic]
```

## Tags Strategy

- 3-5 broad tags: stickman animation, animated story, motivational video
- 3-5 specific tags: [exact topic], [related topics]
- 1-2 competitor channel names (for discoverability)
- Total: 8-12 tags

## Shorts Optimization (Vertical)

- 15-60 seconds ideal
- Hook in first 1 second (no build-up)
- Text overlays for sound-off viewing
- Loop-friendly ending (connects back to opening)
- No end screen or subscribe CTA (breaks the loop)

## Audio Guidelines

- **Music at -18dB during narration** — audible but not competing
- **Music at -6dB during pauses** — fills the gap
- **No silence > 1.5s** — feels like a technical error on YouTube
- **Consistent voice volume** — normalize narration to -14 LUFS
